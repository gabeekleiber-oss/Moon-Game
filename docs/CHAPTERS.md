# CHAPTERS (beats + mechanic). Owner roles in parentheses. Details: docs/source/BRIEF.pdf Part I.

1. **The Fourteenth of September** - Wisconsin living room (Slice)
   Free-look tutorial ~30s -> Sweaty Man bursts in -> finger to lips -> tear open damp letter -> full-screen letter (rhyme)
   -> timed boot-kick -> fetch broom -> thrust handle into TV twice (stops song, then topples) -> "end of my life as I knew it." Flea +25.
2. **Burning Bridges, Drowning Phones** (Slice) - Throwing mini-game: quit job by phone, throw phone into Mary Sue's fish tank, throw the tank
   (power meter, physics, shatter). Load family into the Taurus; letter takes passenger seat, buckled.
3. **The Road South** (Slice) - Stylized night driving game (chase cam). Ruts/hills/jumps, score, racoons, trashcans, rhyming signs,
   left-lane bonus, root-grab hazard near trees, the Inn (gag failure; right choice = drive on), Elsa's buzzing voice with hold-to-listen,
   absurd odometer, dawn arrival card: 5:00 AM San Antonio.
4. **The House in the Clearing** (Mansion) - Aerial approach, hand control at car, bound up the steps, hallway of locked rhyming-plaque doors
   (exactly one open per hallway), staircase to the dome.
5. **The Moon With an Eye** (Mansion) - Dome room, giant moon, eye opens. Verbatim dialogue. Tone choices affect pupil + Flea. Stare-down mini-game.
6. **The First Night of Rooms** (Mansion) - Dark stumble + fumble system, vase shatter, find switch, 12-arm bird lantern, Mary Sue on rug,
   birds: "Leave the girl." / "Make me." Chase upstairs (left, right, left), ~60 birds, closet (throws Mary Sue; drains a heart),
   bird chant, the Gust cyclone mini-game (~15s dodge).
7. **Thirteen Candles** (Mansion) - Maroon hallway -> Wisconsin kitchen. Cake-cutting (Frank, Elsa, each bird on the kite, Christopher last).
   Rhythm-game song (original lyrics/tune). Mary Sue dissolves into Elsa; Elsa thinner/duller. No cheap humour. Frank/Clement exchange.
   Corridor morphs into forest path.
8. **The Lake** (Dream/Day) - Glassy lake, Clement in canoe. Father-argument. Choice-driven rhyme monologue. Memory flash (Edith's truck).
   "God damn you, father." Grackle, fishing tension-bar mini-game (patience). Dying wish. Fade to white: "Hello, son." No music first 60s.
9. **Morning at the Gronfiser's** (Dream/Day) - Wake, straighten 7 pillows, painting hallway (pope, Beatles + 3-5 originals), duck chandeliers,
   20-seat table conversation game, the Gronfiser Verse (animated storybook), the Hard Question ("I left... when they both died.").
10. **The Inventory** (Assets/Audio) - Treasure Notepad exploration: Military Gallery (12+ armor), Bird Room, Statue Room. The Flea Takeover
    (Flea -> ~85, fully manic visuals).
11. **The Melting Spree** (Assets/Audio) - Rally family (bag-looting timer) -> Elsa confrontation -> axe/chop rhythm + whisky sours -> East Wing
    cobwebs -> carry the pot (physics, stair tumble) -> light fire -> feed fire (5 chandeliers, 9 dining sets, necklace) -> flute dance
    (distorting) -> collapse into bed beside Elsa; moon's eye opens. Credits flourish optional.
