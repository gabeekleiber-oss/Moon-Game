# CANON (do not violate)

## Facts
* Story begins **September 14**, during the annual rerun of *The Sound of Music* (pastiche only; no real songs; no real likenesses).
* The TV: a **27-inch RCA CRT** (the "Carrington family" television), hit **twice** with a **broom handle**.
* The Sweaty Man: bathrobe, barefoot, long scraggly beard, a very long finger pressed to the narrator's lips, a damp letter "baking like the sun".
* The letter is from **Joshua Primo, "the Gronfiser"**, after **thirteen years** of unanswered letters. Wisconsin -> San Antonio.
* Family: **Christopher** (narrator, first person), **Elsa Ray** (wife; blue eyes), **Mary Sue** (daughter, turns 13), **Frank** (son). (A stray "John" in the source's birthday scene is a typo: it is Frank.)
* **1994 Ford Taurus**; the letter rides in the passenger seat; the phone and **Mary Sue's fish tank** are thrown.
* Arrival **5 AM**, San Antonio: oak and red brick house, a grassy clearing, thick forest, many windows, chimneys galore. Hot, no snow.
* The Errante (the moon's eye) is **green and blue**. "Thirty five years since we last spoke, to the day."
* **Twelve** bird-nest arms on the lantern; **seven** pillows (the Gronfiser's seal); a **twenty-seat** table with Christopher **eighteen** seats from Frank; **thirteen** candles.
* Statues: Joshua Primo (elephant staff), Crumpis (baboon), Crumpalump (tiger), 8 ft; **Curt** (loon), 3 ft, the very first Gronfiser. Paintings: the pope licking a golden boot; the Gronfiser with the Beatles, a trombone, a crowd of 300,000.
* Clement (Christopher's father, dead; Frank is his image), Edith (his mother), and a sister exist. Dying wish: take your own children fishing.
* Melted: **five chandeliers, nine dining sets, a necklace**. The pot is painted with elephants. The flute is French.
* Ending of this game: Christopher collapses into bed beside Elsa Ray. Final image: the moon's eye opens in the window. (The source story continues beyond this game.)

## Ambiguity rules (NEVER resolve)
* The Errante claims to be Christopher's once-closest friend AND his uncle, father and the Gronfiser. Never confirm or deny. No cutscene, label, sound or codex entry may say what he is.
* Clement never speaks until the final "Hello, son." Never explain whether the lake is memory, gift or lesson.
* Frank is never explained as ghost, vision or real boy.
* "When they both died" is never clarified. Edith's flight is never explained.
* **Add no lore the story doesn't contain.** (Physical detail of places and objects is world-building; backstory is not.)

## Tone and horror
* A sincere, cheerful, rhyming, unreliable narrator; comedy and dread are the same thing. The *world* gets worse while he keeps smiling.
* **Psychological horror first, earned shocks second** (the finger, the eye, the face, "Hello, son", "I left. When they both died."). The shocks are listed in `docs/ACTS.md`; add none without approval. No gore for its own sake. No strobing (max 3 flashes/s). Reduced Motion alternatives for everything that moves.
* Real people named in the story's *words* stay in the words; **never depict a real person's likeness**.
