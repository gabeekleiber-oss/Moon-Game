“How do you do?
You are my nephew. 
I have not seen you since you turned twenty two,
I have spent every moment of the last thirteen years,
Writing letters I hoped would one day reach your ears
But never once, and I do not know why
You could never seem to quite muster a reply
No matter
I have died a great death and been sent on my way
But my riches on earth need a pocket to stay
So to make this quite short 
For I don’t feel like writing
I, Joshua Primo, the Gronfiser, am inviting
You to come down south, to my great big mansion
And sell off your home up in chilly wiscansin
Live in my home and live off of my wealth
So here’s the address and to you, a good health”
